## Continued-context adapter

A whole natural book may use the unchanged v1 coordinator directly. Large books
can use `scripts/continued_context.py` after independent review of the adapter
and each actual cut. The adapter never relaxes the coordinator's context-closed
packet requirement. It adds validated previously completed context to that scope
and records which IDs are owned new work versus inherited context.

The private cut review is exactly `{version:1, verdict:"pass",
sourceCanonicalSha256, ownedUnitIds, additionalContextUnitIds, detail}`. The
source digest is canonical JSON (sorted keys/compact UTF-8). Owned IDs are one
contiguous occurrence range in exact authority order. Additional context captures
semantic dependencies beyond mechanical contextIds, such as an event narrative
or sura introduction. A fresh source-based reviewer must judge those dependencies;
a passing shape or chapter heading alone cannot approve a cut.

`plan` recursively closes governing contexts and requires inherited context to
precede new work. `bind_context` validates each earlier independent companion,
its source/English/isnad bindings and append-only name-ledger compatibility.
Inherited source units must equal the qualified current book authority exactly.
Conflicting revisions, missing companions, changed formula inventories or name
repurposing hold admission. Its source scope and private manifest are bound in
the production profile alongside this adapter and the actual cut review.

The fresh translator receives approved bilingual context as a frozen carry,
not as a new English witness or fresh translation assignment. It translates only
owned new units and returns inherited English/formula targets verbatim in the
closed v1 output. The critic/audit independently checks the new material in that
context. Call `validate_carried` on blind, adjudicated and submitted outputs and
on the final graph; inherited Arabic/English/formulas/structures must not drift.
A fresh extractor copies frozen inherited graphs exactly while extracting all
new occurrences and branching paths; its independent audit checks the complete
packet alignment. A valid v1 stage submission alone cannot bypass the adapter.

Each live claim lists the full context-closed packet; do not run overlapping
cohorts of the same book simultaneously. Close the earlier claim after verified
completion, then reuse context explicitly in the next claim. Completed collection
coverage counts only owned IDs. Cumulative projection deduplicates inherited IDs
by exact source/English/isnad identity, retains their original canonical revision,
and rejects conflicts rather than silently selecting a new rendition. A context
unit is never a second source occurrence or extra progress.
