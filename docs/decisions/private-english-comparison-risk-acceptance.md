# Owner decision: private English comparison

Date: 2026-11-15. Scope: this Sahih al-Bukhari project.

The owner authorizes private comparison with existing English translations,
including AI-assisted processing, in support of this project's independently
authored, commercially intended translation. After a reasonable check finds no
explicit restriction applicable to the intended use, proceed even if affirmative
permission cannot be found. Record the basis exactly as:

> permission unconfirmed; owner accepts risk

Do not ask the owner to approve that same uncertainty again. This is the owner's
operating and risk-acceptance decision, not a license from a rights holder or a
claim of legal clearance. It does not establish that a work is uncopyrighted.

## Source-specific evidence

For each proposed witness (an English translation used for comparison), record
its translator, edition, source, exact artifact hash, attribution, intended use,
and the terms checked, with URLs and review date. Keep source text and detailed
comparison evidence in approved private storage. Record missing or inaccessible
terms honestly; do not describe unread terms as reviewed or absence of an
identified restriction as affirmative permission. A reasonable review of
accessible source and publisher terms is sufficient; an exhaustive permission
search or a rights-holder reply is not a prerequisite under this decision.

Respect explicit restrictions applicable to the proposed use or access method,
including restrictions on commercial use, automated access or AI processing.
This decision does not override those restrictions or authorize bypassing access
controls. Hold the affected source or access method and continue with eligible
alternatives. If new evidence reveals an applicable restriction, reassess the
affected use and preserve the earlier decision evidence.

## Accuracy and publication boundaries

Rights-risk acceptance does not qualify a translation's accuracy. Identify and
assess the actual edition, provenance, coverage, omissions and numbering before
admitting it as a witness. Preserve the Arabic-first workflow: freeze the
independent draft and Arabic critique before English comparison. Use comparison
to flag possible discrepancies; resolve them against Arabic authority with the
existing independent adjudication and audit requirements. English agreement
alone is not proof, and unavailable evidence must remain marked unavailable.

This authorization covers private comparison, not republication of another
translator's English. Independently author the project's English; do not copy
or closely paraphrase witness expression into the release. Existing attribution,
publication, third-party rights and human-review disclosures remain in force.
The project's content license does not relicense third-party translations.

## Relationship to existing policies and profiles

For new private English comparison work in this project, this owner decision
supersedes the affirmative processing/commercial-permission prerequisite in the
[adoption policy](../translation/bukhari-adoption.md),
[quality workflow](../translation/quality-workflow.md) and
[stage contracts](../translation/stage-contracts.md). Other source qualification,
quality, evidence and publication requirements continue to apply.

Preserve historical policies, profiles and receipts that are bound by hashes.
Future comparison integration must bind this decision and accurately distinguish
owner-accepted uncertainty from confirmed external permission. Use a separately
versioned, reviewed implementation if needed; do not silently reinterpret old
rights flags, fabricate permission evidence or bypass validation. Routine work
within this decision does not need another owner approval for the same risk.

This documentation change does not enable comparison in existing profiles or
start a translation run. Later source preparation and translation remain paused
under the [through-Prayer decision](bukhari-through-prayer-working-2.md).
