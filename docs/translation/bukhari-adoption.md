# Bukhari adoption, version 1

## Authorized deliverable and checkpoints

The objective is the complete English translation, including headings, chains,
comments and repeated reports. The first end-to-end context-closed section is a
verification checkpoint, not completion of the collection. Perform deterministic
whole-transcript inventory first, then independent review of its importer and
first-section boundaries before semantic work. Expand only after the first private
candidate satisfies the ordered workflow. No arbitrary batching limit is imposed.

## Text authority and alignment

Use OpenITI JK000110 at the commit and raw hash in bukhari-source-decision.md.
A normalized structured authority records stable occurrence IDs, source locations,
exact Arabic after explicitly logged mechanical removal of mARkdown furniture,
context IDs and formula spans. Preserve immutable raw bytes. Every retained unit
maps to raw line locations; removals and normalization have an auditable ledger.
Do not silently repair Arabic. Source anomalies hold affected units only.

Headings and unnumbered authorial comments belong to the book. Preserve report
isnads together with matn unless independently validated splitting is available;
a report containing a chain is not permission to omit its chain. Printed numbers
are metadata, not identity, and retain duplicates as separate occurrences. Text
inventory is not certification of correspondence to a printed edition.

The source audit identifies corpus-wide anomalies; each translation scope also
requires semantic source/formula review. Never equate regular-expression counts
with complete scholarly audit. Uncertain formula referents require correction or
an explicit hold before admitting the affected scope. Preserve exact Arabic
formula occurrences in English as required by the generic contract.

## Execution methods and evidence

Use the adopted method, openai / gpt-6.1-sol / low, for coordinator
and separately launched first-turn workers, with forkTurns none. This adoption
records the operating method, not a calibration or quality superiority claim.
Execution qualification remains pending authentic host captures. Verify actual host metadata and exact method agreement before recording stages.
Every semantic stage has a distinct session. The blind translator and Arabic
critic receive no English witness. Do not reuse coordinator English as a blind
translation. English comparison is disabled: no witness has been qualified for
the exact use. Resolve Arabic findings directly or with separately registered
witnesses; record genuine uncertainty instead of inventing evidence.

CLI authentication may be unavailable. The GitHub connector may supply the same
live issue claim data to the coordinator library's prepare/check_claim/submit
functions. Fetch all open relevant assignment issues immediately before every
claim/prepare/submit, retain the actual connector response privately, and recheck
overlap after creation. Never fake a live claim or patch out its validation.
A snapshot proves state only at the captured time, not an atomic lock.

## Private candidates and release

The owner's risk decision permits private processing and translation under the
stated public-domain-text rationale. It is not a grant of OpenITI rights. Do not
assert a publicEligible audit while a substantive rights defect remains. A boolean
profile cannot express this distinction: production admission must document the
reviewed source-specific rationale and residual risks before any such flags are
set. Private drafts may be held outside admission with their status explicit.
Do not modify generic rights checks to obtain a passing result.

No public text publication, canonical promotion, human scholarly review, or
third-party licensing is conferred by this setup. Existing immutable fixtures
remain synthetic. New project content is reserved pending an explicit licensing
decision. A failure to qualify public eligibility must hold submission without
blocking the text-only audit or independent private draft.

### Scope-specific interpretation

The opening-section candidate admission rationale is recorded in
bukhari-source-decision.md. Following independent source review, eligible profile
flags may express that narrow decision with disclosed residual risk. They do not
express legal certainty or permission from OpenITI. A scoped structured authority
must bind only the units actually reviewed; a whole-book audit cannot authorize
unreviewed later units. Preserve excluded/context boundaries and frozen raw pins.


## Initial adopted configuration

project.config.json registers the project producer and owner reviewer/promoter roles.
The owner-reviewer ID denotes a potential reviewer, not completed human review.
Canonical promotion is unused. Unreviewed public working releases are disallowed during
adoption. This configuration flag does not disable all release states; actual
book-text release is held by project policy because no distribution contract exists yet. This does not
block private candidate production; adopting actual text release is a separate,
reviewed distribution change. The original example configuration is retained
as a synthetic template and is not operational project authority.
